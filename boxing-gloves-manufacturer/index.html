02Inside the Making

How Our Boxing Gloves Are Made

A glove looks simple from the outside. Its quality is decided in six production stages — this is what happens to every pair we manufacture, and what a serious buyer should expect any manufacturer to explain.

1 · Pattern & Cutting

Each glove style and weight starts from its own pattern set. Panels are marked and cut from the chosen material — genuine cowhide leather or synthetic — so the shell fits the padding build it was designed for, not a generic shape stretched to size.

2 · Padding Build

Layered foam is shaped over the knuckle area: softer layers against the hand for comfort, denser layers outside to absorb impact, formed to keep the fist in a natural position. Sparring builds carry more padding; bag gloves are firmer and more compact.

3 · Stitching

Panels are joined on industrial and post-bed sewing machines with reinforced seams at the stress points. Stitch quality is what decides whether a glove survives years of daily training or splits in its first season — it is checked pair by pair.

4 · Thumb, Lining & Grip

An attached thumb is fitted to help prevent thumb injuries and eye pokes. Inside, a smooth moisture-friendly lining goes in, with a grip bar where the design calls for one, and ventilation options for heavy training use.

5 · Cuff & Branding

The closure is finished to your specification — traditional lace-up for a locked-in fight fit, or a wide hook-and-loop cuff for fast training use and wrist support. Your branding is applied here: printed, embroidered or as a woven label.

6 · Checking & Packing

Every finished pair is checked by hand — stitching, shape, weight, finish and branding — against the approved sample and specification. Only then is it packed, in your packaging if your order calls for it, and cartoned for export.

04Buyer's Guide

Choosing the Right Boxing Gloves Manufacturer

Whether you end up ordering from us or from another factory, these are the checks that separate a real manufacturer from a reseller — and a glove that lasts from one that doesn't.

01

Confirm It Is the Actual Factory

Ask where the gloves are cut and stitched, and ask for real production photos. A manufacturer answers with its own floor and machines; a trading company answers with a catalogue. Registration you can verify — ours is with the Sialkot Chamber of Commerce & Industry and Pakistan FBR — is the other half of this check.

02

Insist on Samples Before Bulk

Never commit to bulk production from photos alone. A proper manufacturer makes a sample to your specification first, so you can test materials, padding, fit and stitching yourself — and bulk production is then held to that approved sample.

03

Get the Materials Declared

"Leather" should mean a stated leather type and grade, and the padding build should be described, not hidden. Declared materials are what make two quotations comparable — and what keeps your second order identical to your first.

04

Everything in Writing

Specification, branding, payment schedule and shipping terms should all be written into your quotation before production begins. If a supplier will not put it in writing, that tells you how problems will be handled later.

05

Judge the Communication

You will work with this factory for years if the first order goes well. Direct answers, honest limits and clear timelines before you pay anything are the best preview of the relationship after you pay.

06

Plan for Reorders

Ask how your specification is kept and matched on reorders. The value of a manufacturer compounds over repeat orders — consistent patterns, materials and branding are what turn a first order into a range. For own-brand production, see our private label boxing gloves manufacturer page.

Infinitum Boxing is a boxing gloves manufacturer based in Sialkot, Pakistan. What is the difference between a gloves manufacturer and a wholesaler or trading company?

A manufacturer makes the gloves: it controls the materials, patterns, padding, stitching and quality checks, and can change any of them to your specification. A wholesaler or trading company buys finished gloves from factories and resells them, so designs, materials and quality are limited to whatever it can source. Buying from the manufacturer gives you specification control, consistent reorders and factory pricing without a middleman's margin.

What types of boxing gloves do you manufacture?

We manufacture the full range a fight-gear buyer needs: all-purpose training gloves, sparring gloves with extra padding, professional and lace-up fight gloves, bag gloves for heavy bag and pad work, competition gloves, and kids' and youth gloves. Every type is produced across the standard weights from 8oz to 20oz, in genuine leather or synthetic constructions.

How are your boxing gloves made?

Production starts with patterns cut for each glove style and weight. Layered foam padding is shaped over the knuckle area, panels are stitched on industrial and post-bed machines, the attached thumb and lining are fitted, and the cuff — lace-up or hook-and-loop — is finished with your branding. Every pair is then checked by hand for stitching, weight, shape and finish before packing.

Should my gloves be leather or synthetic?

Genuine cowhide leather lasts longest, feels natural in the hand and suits pro-style, sparring and premium retail ranges. Quality synthetic and microfiber constructions cost less, hold colour and texture very consistently, resist moisture and suit gym, fitness, kids' and high-volume ranges. Many buyers run both.
