Custom boxing gloves, made to your brief

If you are launching a boxing brand, supplying gyms or stocking a retail shelf, the gloves need to carry your name and match your standards. We build custom boxing gloves to your design in our own unit in Sialkot, Pakistan’s long-established sporting goods hub, and handle the export side directly, so you talk to the people who make the product.

Every order starts with a conversation, not a catalogue. You tell us the glove type, quantity and branding. We confirm the details, make a sample, and move to bulk once you are happy.

    When the order is ready, the remaining 50% is paid and we ship by the method you chose.

Custom boxing gloves FAQs

What is the minimum order quantity for custom boxing gloves?

Our MOQ starts at 10 pairs or pieces, depending on the product. Tell us the glove type and we will confirm the exact minimum for it.

How long does a sample take?

When there is no rush, a sample usually takes about one week. Bulk production time depends on the quantity, so we confirm it when we send your quotation.

Do you charge for samples?

Yes, samples are paid. For serious buyers we can discuss the arrangement personally, and in some cases it is possible to charge only the shipping. Message us with your project and we will tell you what works.

Can I use my own logo, colours and packaging?

Yes. Full customisation is available in every material we work with, including logo, colours, design details and packaging. We also work as a private-label manufacturer, so you can sell the gloves under your own brand.

Which types of boxing gloves can you make?

All the main types: training gloves, sparring gloves, bag gloves and MMA gloves. Send us the use, weight and design you have in mind and we will plan the sample.

What are your payment terms?

We work on 50% advance to start the order and the remaining 50% when the order is ready, before it ships.

How do you ship, and have you exported to Europe?

You choose the shipping method. We can offer the options that fit your budget and timeline. We have shipped to many countries, including France, Belgium and South Korea.

Is Infinitum Boxing a registered business?

Yes. We are registered with the Sialkot Chamber of Commerce, registered with the FBR (NTN) and bank verified.
