Buyer Questions

Private Label Gloves — FAQ

The questions brands, gyms and retailers ask us first. Anything else, ask us directly on WhatsApp — you will get an answer from the factory, not a call centre.

Can you put our own logo on the boxing gloves?

Yes. As a private label manufacturer we apply your logo and artwork to the gloves using the branding method that suits your design, and we can also brand the packaging. Share your logo files or design brief and we will prepare your sample exactly to that specification.

Can we order a sample before bulk production?

Yes. Samples are made before bulk production so you can check the materials, fit, stitching and branding first. Include your sample requirements in your quote request and the sample approach, cost and timing for your exact product will be confirmed in your written quotation.

What is the minimum order quantity for private label gloves?

There is no single fixed number for every order. Quantities are flexible and depend on the glove style, materials and level of customisation you need. Tell us what you are planning and we will quote for your exact requirement, so growing brands and first orders get fair support.

Which materials and colours can we choose from?

Gloves are made to your specification, including genuine leather and quality synthetic options, padding and lining choices, and your own colour combinations. If you have a reference glove or a tech pack, send it over and we will match the production to it as closely as possible.

Can you make custom packaging with our branding?

Yes. Retail-ready packaging can be produced with your branding, so your gloves arrive shelf-ready with your name on the box as well as on the product. Packaging requirements are confirmed with you before production begins.

Do you ship private label orders to the USA and Europe?

Yes. We supply buyers in the USA, the UK, Europe and other markets worldwide. Shipping options are selected for your destination and schedule, and the agreed arrangements are confirmed in writing before production starts.
