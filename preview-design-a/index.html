02Materials & Construction

Leather or Synthetic — Built Properly Either Way

The two decisions that define a glove are the outer material and what sits inside it. Here is how the options compare.

PropertyGenuine Cowhide LeatherSynthetic / Microfiber
Feel & fitNatural, supple hand feel; moulds to the hand over timeUniform, consistent texture from the first wear
DurabilityLongest service life; the standard for pro and sparring rangesVery good for training volume; resists moisture and sweat well
FinishPremium look and ageing character; takes print and embroidery wellExact colour matching across large runs; smooth, even surface for bold artwork
Cost positionHigher — a premium retail price pointLower — suits gym, fitness, starter and promotional price points
Best forFight brands, pro-style and sparring gloves, premium retailGym chains, fitness boxing, kids' ranges, events and promotions

Padding & Protection

Gloves are built with layered foam padding over the knuckles — softer layers against the hand for comfort, denser layers outside for impact absorption — shaped to keep the fist in a natural, safe position. Sparring constructions carry extra padding; bag gloves are firmer and more compact. Your padding build is set in your specification and proven in your sample.

Construction Details

An attached thumb helps prevent thumb injuries and eye pokes. Choose lace-up closure for a traditional, locked-in fight fit or a wide hook-and-loop cuff for fast on-off training use and strong wrist support. Panels are stitched with reinforced seams, the lining is smooth and moisture-friendly, and grip bars and ventilation can be added where your design calls for them.

Branding Methods

Your logo can be screen-printed, embroidered or applied as a woven label on the cuff — the method is chosen to suit your artwork and material, and colour matching is done to your brand palette or Pantone references. What you approve on the sample is what bulk production follows.
