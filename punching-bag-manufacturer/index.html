02 — ANATOMY

How a Bag That Lasts Is Built

A punching bag takes more punishment than almost any other product in a gym, and bags fail at predictable points — seams, straps, closures. This is the construction we manufacture to, stage by stage, and what a serious buyer should ask any manufacturer to explain.

01

Pattern & Shell Panels

Each design starts from its own pattern set: long shell panels marked and cut from the chosen material — genuine leather, synthetic leather or heavy-duty canvas — sized to the length and diameter of the finished bag.

02

Inner Lining

A lining is fitted inside the shell so filling settles evenly and the bag holds its shape under daily impact, instead of lumping or sagging at the seams after a few months of gym use.

03

Reinforced Stitching

Panels are joined on industrial sewing machines with reinforced seams at the stress points. Stitch quality decides whether a bag survives years of commercial training or splits in its first season — it is checked bag by bag as production runs.

04

Hanging System, Anchored In

The hanging system is built into the bag's structure: heavy webbing straps with metal D-rings, or chain sets, to your specification. This is the highest-stress part of any bag, so it is never a surface patch sewn on afterwards.

05

Branding & Closure

Your branding is applied along the bag body — the largest logo surface in the gym. The closure is then finished: a secure opening that lets the end user fill, top up or re-fill the bag through its working life.

06

Checked & Packed

Every finished bag is checked by hand — stitching, shape, hardware and branding — against the approved sample and specification. Unfilled bags are folded and cartoned for export, so a bulk order ships at a fraction of the freight cost of filled bags.

03 — THE FREIGHT DECISION

Filled vs Unfilled: Do the Maths First

The single biggest cost mistake in bag importing is paying international freight on filling. Here is the honest comparison we walk every new buyer through.

For Export Orders

Unfilled — Fill at Destination

  • You ship the manufactured bag only — folded, compact cartons
  • Freight is charged on a light, dense shipment, not on 40+ kg of filling per bag
  • Fill locally with shredded textile or fabric offcuts — the standard gym filling, cheap everywhere
  • End users can top up or re-fill through the closure for the bag's whole life
  • Your landed cost per bag stays low, which protects your retail margin
VS

For Regional Orders

Filled — Ready to Hang

  • Bags arrive filled to the agreed weight, ready to hang on delivery
  • Sensible only where freight distances are short enough that the weight is affordable
  • No local filling step for your team or your customers
  • Weight and firmness are set at the factory to your specification
  • Discuss it in your enquiry and we will quote both ways so you can compare

07 — BUYER QUESTIONS

Punching Bag Manufacturer — FAQ

The questions buyers ask us first when they are looking for a punching bag manufacturer. Anything else, ask us directly on WhatsApp — you will get an answer from the factory, not a call centre.

Who is a punching bag manufacturer in Pakistan that sells factory-direct?

Infinitum Boxing is a punching bag manufacturer based in Sialkot, Pakistan. We cut, stitch and finish our bags in our own factory and sell directly to brands, gyms, retailers and distributors in the USA, Europe and worldwide — you contract with the factory itself, not with a trading company or middleman reselling someone else's production.

What types of punching bags do you manufacture?

We manufacture the bag range a fight-gym buyer needs: classic heavy punching bags for boxing training, long Muay Thai banana bags for kicks and knees, angle and uppercut bags for hooks and uppercut work, and bouncing bags for rebound and combination drills. Every type is produced to your length, diameter and material specification, with or without your branding.

Should I order punching bags filled or unfilled?

For export orders, unfilled is the practical choice: a filled heavy bag is mostly weight, so shipping filled bags internationally means paying freight on filling you can source locally — shredded textile and fabric offcuts are the standard gym filling. We manufacture and ship bags unfilled with a secure closure, ready to fill at your end, which keeps your landed cost and your retail margin healthy. Filled supply can be discussed for regional orders where freight makes sense.

What materials are your punching bags made from?

Your choice, declared in writing before production: genuine leather for the longest service life and a premium retail range; synthetic leather for a consistent finish, moisture resistance and a sharper price point; or heavy-duty canvas for a traditional, hard-wearing training bag. The inner lining, panel build and stitching specification are agreed with the material, so two quotations can be compared like for like.

How is a quality punching bag constructed?

A bag that survives commercial gym use is built, not just sewn: long shell panels cut to the design, reinforced stitching at the seams and stress points, a strong hanging system — webbing straps with metal D-rings, or chain sets — anchored into the bag's structure rather than a surface patch, a lined interior so filling settles evenly, and a secure top or bottom closure. Every bag is checked by hand for stitching, shape and hardware before packing.

Yes. OEM and private label production is a core part of what we do, and a punching bag is the best branding surface in a gym — your logo can run large along the bag body, in your colours, with your packaging and labels. Send us your artwork with your quote request and the branding is built into your specification before the sample is made.

What should I check when choosing a punching bag manufacturer?

Check five things: that you are dealing with the actual factory and not a reseller; how the seams and hanging straps are constructed, because that is where bags fail; that materials are declared honestly (leather grade, synthetic type, lining); that samples are made to your specification before bulk; and that the business is registered and verifiable, as we are with the Sialkot Chamber of Commerce & Industry and Pakistan FBR, with pricing and terms in writing before production starts.
