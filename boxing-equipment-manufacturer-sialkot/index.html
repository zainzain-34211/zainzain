05How Ordering Works

From Enquiry to Your Door

One process covers the whole range, whether your order is a single glove style or a full facility fit-out — specification, branding, payment and shipping are all confirmed in writing before bulk production begins.

  1. 1

    Share Your Brief

    Send your product list, branding, materials and planned quantities. A logo file, a reference product, a tech pack or a simple written list all work — message us on WhatsApp or email.

  2. 2

    Get Your Written Quote

    You receive one quotation prepared for your exact requirement — every line in your order priced on the same basis, including the sample approach and the payment and shipping terms, documented in advance.

  3. 3

    Approve the Samples

    We produce samples of the items you want to check. You verify materials, construction, fit and branding, request adjustments if needed, and approve before bulk production starts.

  4. 4

    Production & Quality Checks

    Your order is manufactured in our Sialkot factory. Materials, stitching, sizing and branding are checked against the approved samples and agreed specification during production and again before packing.

Packaging

Products are packed to your requirement — individual polybags or your branded retail boxes, with hangtags and labels where your range needs them — then cartons are prepared for export transport.

Payment, Simply Explained

Orders are normally paid by bank transfer against a proforma invoice issued for your confirmed order. The payment schedule for your order is written into your quotation before production begins — agreed, documented, no surprises.

Export & Shipping to the USA & Europe

Shipments leave with the standard export documents — commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin. Shipping terms (for example EXW, FOB or delivered arrangements) are selected for your destination and agreed in your quotation.

07Buyer Questions

Boxing Equipment Manufacturing — FAQ

The questions brands, gyms and distributors ask us first about ordering from a manufacturer in Sialkot. Anything else, ask us directly on WhatsApp — you will get an answer from the factory, not a call centre.

Which boxing equipment manufacturer in Sialkot, Pakistan can I buy from directly?

Infinitum Boxing is a boxing equipment manufacturer based in Sialkot, Pakistan, and you buy from the factory itself — there is no trading company in between. We manufacture boxing gloves, head guards, punching bags, focus pads, protective gear, boxing shoes, championship belts and MMA wear, and supply brands, gyms, retailers and distributors in the USA, Europe and worldwide.

What boxing equipment do you manufacture?

Our range covers the full gym and fight-gear catalogue: boxing gloves and bag gloves, head guards, punching bags and free-standing bags, focus pads and boxing paddles, chest and groin guards, mouth guards, boxing shoes, championship belts, shin guards, and MMA gloves, shorts and training wear. The complete range is listed in our online shop, and every line can be produced to your specification.

Can you produce equipment under our own brand?

Yes. Private label and OEM production is the core of what we do: your logo, your colours and your packaging across gloves, guards, bags, apparel and more. Branding is applied by printing, embroidery or woven labels, depending on the product and your artwork, and branded retail packaging can be produced so your range arrives shelf-ready.

Can we order samples before bulk production?

Yes. Samples are produced before bulk so you can check materials, construction, fit and branding on the actual product. Tell us which products you want sampled when you request your quote, and the sample approach, cost and timing for those items will be confirmed in your written quotation.

What is the minimum order quantity?

There is no single fixed number across the range. Quantities depend on the product, the materials and the level of customisation, so instead of a rigid minimum we quote for your exact requirement. A new brand ordering its first glove style and a distributor ordering across the whole range are quoted on the same basis: your specification, your quantity, your destination.

Can you supply a mixed order across the whole range?

Yes. Mixed orders are normal for us — gyms equipping a full facility and brands launching a complete range often combine gloves, bags, pads, protection and apparel in a single order. Send your list with quantities per line and your quotation will cover the whole order, with samples available for the items you want to check first.

Which materials do you use — leather or synthetic?

Both, depending on the product and your market. Genuine cowhide leather is used where service life and a premium finish matter most, such as pro-style gloves and head guards. Quality synthetic and microfiber options keep costs lower, hold colour consistently and suit high-volume gym, fitness and promotional ranges. Your quotation will recommend the right material for each product in your order.

Can you match a reference sample or a tech pack?

Yes. Send a reference product you already sell, or a tech pack with measurements, materials and artwork, and we will produce a sample matched to it as closely as possible. Anything we cannot match exactly is confirmed with you in writing before bulk production begins, so there are no surprises in the finished order.

Do you make custom sizes and colours?

Yes. Gloves are produced across the standard 8oz to 20oz range with kids' and youth sizes available, head guards and protective gear come in graded sizes, and bags can be made to the lengths your gym needs. Colours are matched to your brand palette or Pantone references, and your exact size and colour run is confirmed in your quotation.

What payment methods do you accept?

Orders are normally paid by bank transfer against a proforma invoice issued for your confirmed order. The exact payment schedule for your order is written into your quotation before production begins, so everything is agreed and documented in advance — there are no surprises later.

What export documents do you provide with a shipment?

Shipments are prepared with the standard export documentation — commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin — and any additional documents your destination or your freight forwarder requires can be discussed when you request your quote. Shipping terms (for example EXW, FOB or delivered options) are agreed in your written quotation.

Do you ship to the USA and Europe?

Yes. We supply buyers in the USA, the UK, across Europe and in other markets worldwide. Shipping options are selected for your destination and schedule, cartons are prepared for export transport, and the agreed arrangements are confirmed in writing before production starts.
