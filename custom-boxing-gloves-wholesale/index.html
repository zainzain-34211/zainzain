03Materials & Construction

Leather or Synthetic — Built Properly Either Way

Wholesale buyers choose the material that fits their market and price point. Both options are produced in the same factory, to the same construction standard — here is how they compare for a stocked range.

Cost positionHigher — a premium retail price pointLower — suits gym, fitness, starter and promotional price points

Padding & Protection

Gloves are built with layered foam padding over the knuckles — softer layers against the hand for comfort, denser layers outside for impact absorption — shaped to keep the fist in a natural, safe position. Sparring constructions carry extra padding; bag gloves are firmer and more compact. Your range's padding build is set in your specification and proven in your sample.

Construction Details

An attached thumb helps prevent thumb injuries and eye pokes. Choose lace-up closure for a traditional, locked-in fight fit or a wide hook-and-loop cuff for fast on-off training use and strong wrist support. Panels are stitched with reinforced seams, the lining is smooth and moisture-friendly, and grip bars and ventilation can be added where your design calls for them.

Branding Methods

Your logo can be screen-printed, embroidered or applied as a woven label on the cuff — or left off entirely for unbranded stock. The method is chosen to suit your artwork and material, and colour matching is done to your brand palette or Pantone references. What you approve on the sample is what bulk production follows.

08Buyer Questions

Custom Boxing Gloves Wholesale — FAQ

The questions retailers, gyms and distributors ask us first. Anything else, ask us directly on WhatsApp — you will get an answer from the factory, not a call centre.

Can we buy custom boxing gloves wholesale without our own logo?

Yes. Wholesale gloves can be supplied unbranded for resale, or with your logo and branding if you want your own label. Tell us which route you want in your quote request and we will price and produce exactly that version.

Who do you supply wholesale gloves to?

We supply retailers, online stores, gyms, boxing clubs, distributors and fight-gear brands in the USA, the UK, Europe and other markets worldwide. Whether you stock one style or a full range, your order is quoted for your exact requirement.

Can we mix glove styles, sizes and colours in one wholesale order?

Yes. A wholesale order can combine styles, sizes and colourways to match what your customers actually buy. Send your mix and we will confirm it in your written quotation before production begins.

Can we get a sample before placing a bulk wholesale order?

Yes. Samples are made before bulk production so you can check materials, fit, stitching and branding first. Include your sample requirements in your quote request and the sample approach for your exact product will be confirmed in your quotation.

Do you offer reorders and restocking for wholesale customers?

Yes. Once your style and specification are approved, reorders are produced to the same specification, so your shelves stay consistent order after order. Restock quantities and timing are confirmed per order in your quotation.

Do you ship wholesale boxing gloves to the USA and Europe?

Yes. We supply buyers in the USA, the UK, Europe and other markets worldwide. Shipping options are selected for your destination and schedule, and the agreed arrangements are confirmed in writing before production starts.

Is there a minimum order quantity for wholesale gloves?

There is no single fixed minimum for every wholesale order. The right quantity depends on the glove styles, materials and branding in your order, so instead of a rigid minimum we quote for your exact requirement. Tell us the styles and quantities you are planning and we will prepare a written quotation around them — including mixed styles and sizes if that is what your range needs.

Which materials can we choose from — leather or synthetic?

Both. Genuine cowhide leather gives the longest service life, a natural feel and a premium finish, which is why premium retail and sparring ranges usually choose it. Quality synthetic and microfiber options cost less per pair, hold colour consistently across large runs and resist moisture well, which suits gym, fitness and high-volume ranges. Tell us your market and price point and we will recommend the right material in your quotation.

What sizes and weights of gloves do you make?

We produce the full standard range from 8oz to 20oz. As a guide: 8oz–10oz for competition and bag work, 12oz for general training, 14oz–16oz for sparring, and 18oz–20oz for heavy sparring and maximum protection. Kids' and youth sizes can also be produced, and wholesale orders can mix weights across the range — your exact size run is confirmed in your quotation.

Can you match a reference sample or a tech pack?

Yes. If you already sell a glove you want to keep, or you have a tech pack with measurements, materials and artwork, send it over and we will produce a sample matched to it as closely as possible, then confirm any differences with you in writing before bulk production.

What payment methods do you accept?

Orders are normally paid by bank transfer against a proforma invoice issued for your confirmed order. The exact payment schedule for your order is written into your quotation before production begins, so everything is agreed and documented in advance — there are no surprises later.

What export documents do you provide with a shipment?

Shipments are prepared with the standard export documentation — commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin — and any additional documents your destination or your freight forwarder requires can be discussed when you request your quote. Shipping terms (for example EXW, FOB or delivered options) are agreed in your written quotation.
